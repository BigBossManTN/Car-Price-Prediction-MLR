Project Description:

This project aims to predict the selling price of used cars based on their specifications such as brand, engine size, horsepower, mileage, and other features. The goal is to build a machine learning model that helps estimate fair market prices for vehicles, supporting better decision-making for car buyers and sellers.

Problem Definition:

The objective of this project is to predict the price of a car (y = price) based on its technical and categorical features such as engine size, horsepower, fuel type, and brand.
This is a regression problem, as the target variable (price) is continuous.
The intended users are car dealers, resellers, and buyers who want to estimate a fair market value for vehicles.
The model’s performance will be evaluated using the following metrics: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and R² Score.
